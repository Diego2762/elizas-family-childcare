The toddler years

Big growth, guided by play

Between one and three, children learn more than at almost any other time. Play is how they do it.

Our toddlers spend their days building towers, singing songs, turning pages, splashing at the sensory table and learning to share, all while soaking up Spanish and English naturally. Right now their brains are wired to absorb language, so hearing two of the world's most spoken languages every day builds an advantage that lasts a lifetime. We gently support big milestones like language explosion, self-feeding and potty training, always at each child's own pace.

Small groups mean fewer meltdowns and more connection: we can catch the frustration before the tears, celebrate every new word, and give the individual attention toddlers thrive on.

What toddlers get with us

Everything in a full, happy day

  • Language explosion, x2. Songs, stories and constant talk in Spanish and English fuel booming vocabularies.
  • Potty-training support. We teach little ones to use the bathroom, patient, encouraging and coordinated with your approach at home.
  • Hands-on learning centers. Blocks, art, pretend play, sensory bins and books to explore.
  • Homemade meals & self-feeding. Family-style meals that build independence and healthy habits.
  • Active outdoor play. Movement, fresh air and gross-motor fun every day.
  • Social & emotional skills. Sharing, turn-taking and naming feelings in a gentle, guided way.

Toddler questions

Good to know

Do you help with potty training?
Yes. We support potty training patiently and consistently, working alongside your routine at home so your toddler feels confident, not pressured.
My toddler only speaks one language, is that okay?
Completely. Children pick up a second language remarkably fast at this age through everyday play and song. Whether your family speaks English, Spanish or both, your toddler will flourish.
How do you handle tantrums and big feelings?
Gently and consistently. Small groups let us stay close, name feelings, and guide toddlers through frustration with patience and warmth.
Are part-time or sibling options available?
We offer flexible options and sibling consideration. See our tuition page or ask us at your tour.
