The preschool years

Learning that feels like play

Three-to-five-year-olds are ready for more, more challenge, more responsibility, more big ideas.

Our preschoolers explore letters and sounds, numbers and patterns, science and art through hands-on projects that feel like fun, not worksheets. They build the focus, curiosity and independence that make kindergarten a joy instead of a shock, all while growing fluent and comfortable in both Spanish and English. Because these are two of the world's most spoken languages, that fluency is a head start that reaches far beyond kindergarten, into new friendships, travel and, one day, their career.

Because our groups stay small, every child gets to lead, to be heard, and to move ahead at the pace that's right for them. No child is lost in a crowd of twenty-four.

Building kindergarten readiness

The skills that matter most

  • Early literacy. Letters, sounds, rhyming and storytelling that grow a love of reading in both languages.
  • Early math & thinking. Counting, sorting, patterns and problem-solving through hands-on play.
  • True bilingualism. Confident everyday Spanish and English, a lifelong head start.
  • Independence & responsibility. Self-help skills, cleanup jobs and following multi-step directions.
  • Creativity & science. Art, music, building and simple experiments that spark curiosity.
  • Social confidence. Cooperation, sharing, resolving conflicts and speaking up in a group.

Preschool questions

Good to know

Will my child be ready for kindergarten?
Yes, that's exactly what these years are for. We build early literacy, math, focus and independence through play, so your child walks into kindergarten confident and eager to learn.
Is it a real bilingual program?
Yes. Spanish and English are woven through the whole day, songs, stories, instructions and conversation, so children grow genuinely comfortable in both. Families who speak only one language at home are very welcome.
Do you offer a pre-K / transitional kindergarten focus?
Our oldest children (4-5) get extra readiness: name writing, letter sounds, early math, and the self-help and listening skills kindergarten teachers look for.
Are part-time or sibling options available?
We offer flexible options and sibling consideration. See our tuition page or ask us at your tour.
