A day with us

A gentle, predictable rhythm

Young children thrive on routine. Our days flow with a calm, consistent rhythm, balancing active play, quiet time, learning and plenty of good food.

  • 7:30-9:00 AM
    Warm arrival & breakfast
    Fruit, cereal, pancakes, eggs and milk to start the day.
  • 9:00 AM
    Circle time
    A book, songs, music, dancing and a hands-on project, in Spanish & English.
  • 10:00 AM
    Diapers, potty time & free play
    Gentle potty-training support at each child's pace, then painting, water play and playdough.
  • 11:30 AM
    Lunch together
    Served family-style, then tidying up and washing hands.
  • 12:45-2:30 PM
    Nap & quiet time
    Cozy beds and rest to recharge.
  • 3:00 PM
    Wake-up & snack
    Fruit, crackers, milk and water.
  • 3:45 PM
    Play & wind-down
    Legos, puzzles and building games together.
  • 4:30 PM
    Story time & going home
    We read a book together and settle in while we wait for families to arrive.

In every program

The same promise, at every age

No matter the stage, these are the things every child receives with us, every single day.

Small groups, low ratios

An intimate home setting means more one-on-one attention.

Play-based learning

Developmentally-guided days that grow with each child.

Bilingual immersion

Spanish & English woven naturally into everyday play.

Safe home environment

A fully childproofed, licensed home you can trust.

What they learn

Math, science, art and music, all through play

Screen-free doesn't mean less learning. Every day quietly covers the building blocks kindergarten expects, taught with hands, songs and curiosity instead of worksheets.

Language & literacy

Letters, sounds, rhymes, stories and real conversation in Spanish and English, every single day.

Early math

Counting, sorting, shapes, sizes and patterns through games, blocks and everyday moments.

Science & discovery

Simple experiments, planting, water and sensory play that turn every “why?” into something to explore.

Art & creativity

Painting, drawing, playdough, collage and open-ended projects children are proud to take home.

Music & movement

Songs, instruments, dancing and rhythm at circle time, a favorite part of the day in both languages.

Motor skills & independence

Climbing, building, cutting, pouring and self-help skills that build real confidence.
