Why parents trust us with their baby

Secure, gentle beginnings

Babies grow best when one loving caregiver truly knows them. Our small-group home is built for exactly that.

Unlike large centers with rotating staff and big infant rooms, we offer consistency and closeness. We follow your baby's own rhythm for feeding and sleep, share photos and notes whenever you ask, and fill the day with the cuddles, tummy time and gentle stimulation that early development depends on.

And because we're a bilingual home, your little one hears Spanish and English from the very start, building the foundation for two languages before they even say their first word. These are two of the most spoken languages in the world, a head start that will keep opening doors for the rest of their life.

What's included for infants

Everything your baby needs in a day

  • Safe-sleep practices. Cribs, back-to-sleep routines and close monitoring aligned with safety guidelines.
  • Your baby's schedule. We follow your feeding, bottle and nap routine, no forcing a rigid timetable.
  • Homemade purees & meals. Fresh, age-appropriate food as your baby begins solids, allergy-aware always.
  • Bilingual songs & talk. Constant gentle narration and singing in Spanish and English.
  • Tummy time & sensory play. Purposeful play that builds strength, senses and curiosity.
  • Updates on request. Just ask and we'll share photos and notes on meals, diapers, naps and milestones.

Infant questions

Good to know

What ages do you take for infant care?
We welcome infants from as early as your family is ready through age 1, when they gently transition into our toddler program.
Do you follow my baby's feeding and nap schedule?
Yes. We follow your baby's individual routine for bottles, meals and sleep, and we keep you updated throughout the day.
Are meals included for infants?
Yes, fresh, homemade, age-appropriate food (including purees as your baby starts solids) is included in tuition, and we accommodate allergies.
Is the infant space bilingual too?
Absolutely. From day one your baby hears Spanish and English through songs, narration and play, the natural foundation for bilingualism.
