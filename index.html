Bilingual hearts, curious minds

Two languages. One loving home.

In a city as diverse as San Jose, growing up bilingual is a gift for life. At Eliza's, Spanish and English aren't a class; they're simply how we live, play and learn together every day.

And these aren't just any two languages. English is the most widely spoken language in the world, and Spanish has the second-most native speakers of any language. Together they reach over 1.5 billion people. Every word your child picks up now opens real doors: more friendships, more travel, stronger brain development and a real head start for school and, one day, their career.

What they learn

Math, science, art and music, all through play

Screen-free doesn't mean less learning. Every day quietly covers the building blocks kindergarten expects, taught with hands, songs and curiosity instead of worksheets.

Language & literacy

Letters, sounds, rhymes, stories and real conversation in Spanish and English, every single day.

Early math

Counting, sorting, shapes, sizes and patterns through games, blocks and everyday moments.

Science & discovery

Simple experiments, planting, water and sensory play that turn every “why?” into something to explore.

Art & creativity

Painting, drawing, playdough, collage and open-ended projects children are proud to take home.

Music & movement

Songs, instruments, dancing and rhythm at circle time, a favorite part of the day in both languages.

Motor skills & independence

Climbing, building, cutting, pouring and self-help skills that build real confidence.
