Tuition questions

Good to know

Are meals really included in the price?
Yes, four homemade meals and snacks a day are part of tuition, with no separate food fee. We cook on site and accommodate allergies.
Do you offer sibling or part-time discounts?
We do. Families with more than one child receive a sibling discount, and we offer flexible part-time schedules plus a small discount for monthly payment. We'll tailor it to your family at the tour.
What do I need to enroll?
After your tour and a spot offer: a registration form, your child's immunization and health records, emergency contacts, and a deposit to reserve the space. We guide you through each step.
When are the best times to apply?
Spots open year-round but fill fastest in spring and late summer. If you have a start date in mind, reach out early so we can plan your child's transition.
Are these prices final?
These are our current weekly rates and include four homemade meals and snacks daily. We'll confirm your exact rate, including any discounts and schedule adjustments, at your tour.
